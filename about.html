About me

A Computer Science graduate building with code and creativity.

I'm a BS Computer Science graduate with a strong interest in WordPress development, app development, and digital content creation — mixing solid technical foundations with a genuine love for the creative side of building things.

My academic journey gave me a solid foundation in software development, and the hands-on projects I took on along the way sharpened both my technical and creative skills — the kind of learning that only really sticks when you're actually building something.

My experience spans WordPress websites, Android app development, and content creation. I've also worked with Adobe Premiere Pro and Photoshop for video editing and design, which lets me approach a project from both a developer's and a designer's point of view — thinking about how something works and how it feels at the same time.

That mix is what I enjoy most: taking a project from a rough idea, through the build, and into something polished enough to actually put in front of people.
